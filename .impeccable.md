## Design Context

### Users
Swimphony is performed by an aquarium owner or installation operator for an audience in a home, gallery, event, café, or application demo. The operator needs reliable controls on the Mac while the audience sees a separate, camera-free projection that makes the goldfish's influence immediately legible.

### Brand Personality
Alive, meditative, and refined. The experience should feel like a living instrument rather than a sensor dashboard: elegant enough for an art installation, with distinct visual presets that can change its atmosphere without losing its identity.

### Aesthetic Direction
An organic nocturnal waterscape led by warm gold light, deep green-black water, lingering trails, and slow dissolves. Projection output is cinematic and sparse. Avoid generic neon gradients, technical HUD clutter, constant spectacle, flashing, cartoon decoration, and joke effects.

### Design Principles
- Make the fish-to-light causality visible without showing the camera feed.
- Keep the golden trail as the unmistakable visual signature.
- Let motion breathe; never flash or stimulate the fish for performance.
- Separate operator controls from the audience projection.
- Treat each visual style as a selectable preset, just like the sound modes.
- Preserve legibility and provide reduced-motion behavior.
