# 11. Camera Shooting Guide

## Required sample video

Record one front-facing video and save it as:

```text
public/demo/goldfish-demo.mp4
```

## Recommended settings

- Duration: 40–60 seconds
- Resolution: 1080p
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Lens: normal wide camera, no digital zoom
- Orientation: landscape, preferably 16:9
- Camera: fixed on a tripod or stable support
- Aquarium: fills most of the frame
- Lighting: constant during the whole clip
- Flash: off

## Reflection reduction

- Place the camera close to perpendicular to the aquarium glass.
- Darken the space immediately behind the camera.
- Use a black cloth around the camera if practical.
- Keep green decorative lights and Hue reflections out of the aquarium image during calibration footage.
- Do not press the lens against wet glass or balance expensive electronics in a position designed by optimism.

## Content coverage

The clip should include ordinary movement across:

- left and right
- top and bottom
- at least one turn
- one short period where the fish is partly hard to see

Do not provoke the fish. Natural motion is enough.
